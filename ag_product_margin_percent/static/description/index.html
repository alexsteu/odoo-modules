How it works

1. Install the module.

There is nothing to set up.

2. Open the product list.

The column Margin % comes right after the sales price.

3. Spot the problems.

A negative margin is shown in red.

Settings

Nothing to configure. To hide the column, use the column picker at the top right of the list.

Good to know

  • Margin % = (sales price − cost) ÷ sales price.
  • The module uses the sales price and the cost of the product form. Pricelists and discounts are not taken into account.
  • A product with a cost of 0 shows a margin of 100 %. Fill in your costs first.
  • The margin is 0 when the sales price is 0.
