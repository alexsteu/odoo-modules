How it works

1. Send your quotation as usual.

The module watches quotations in the Quotation Sent state whose customer has an e-mail address.

2. Odoo reminds the customer.

Once a day, Odoo checks. After the delay you chose with no answer, it sends a short e-mail with a View the quotation button. The e-mail is logged in the chatter.

3. It stops by itself.

No reminder once the customer has answered in the chatter, when the quotation has expired, when the maximum is reached, or when you tick No automatic follow-up on the quotation.

On each sent quotation, Other Info tab: the day of the next reminder, how many were sent, and a box to stop them. Demo data.

Settings

Where: Sales › Configuration › Settings › Quotations & Orders › Automatic quotation follow-up

SettingWhat it doesDefault
Automatic quotation follow-upSwitches the reminders on or off.On
After … daysDays without answer before a reminder. Counted from the day the quotation was sent, then from the last reminder or from your last message to the customer.5
at most … timesMaximum number of reminders for one quotation.2
Only quotations sent sinceQuotations sent before this date are never chased.Day of installation

The text of the e-mail is the template Sales: quotation follow-up. Edit it in Settings › Technical › Email Templates (developer mode).

Good to know

  • The reminders are on as soon as the module is installed. Only quotations sent from that day on are chased.
  • The e-mail is in French for customers whose language is French, in English for all others.
  • Odoo must be able to send e-mails: an outgoing mail server has to be configured.
  • If you upgrade from version 18.0.1.0.0, the e-mail text is kept as it was. To get the new wording, open the template and use Action › Reset Template.
