Automatic Quotation Follow-up

Stop losing deals because nobody followed up on a quotation

Most quotations are not refused, they are simply forgotten. This module sends a polite reminder e-mail to customers who received a quotation and did not answer.

  • Runs every day on its own: quotations in the Quotation Sent state get a reminder after a delay you choose (5 days by default).
  • Maximum number of reminders per quotation (2 by default), then it stops.
  • No reminder for expired quotations, confirmed orders or customers without e-mail.
  • Untick per quotation with No automatic follow-up (Other Info tab).
  • The e-mail contains a button to view and sign the quotation online, and is logged in the chatter.
  • Fully editable e-mail template: Sales: quotation follow-up.

Setup

Sales > Configuration > Settings > Quotations & Orders > Automatic quotation follow-up.
