AI Vendor Bill Reader

Stop typing supplier invoices by hand, also in Odoo Community

Free module by an independent Odoo developer. Need it adapted to your company? See below.

  • Attach the supplier invoice (PDF or photo) to a draft vendor bill and click Read with AI.
  • The AI fills the vendor (found by VAT number or name, created if new), the invoice number, dates, currency and every line with quantity, price and tax.
  • Several bills at once: select them in the list, then Actions > Read with AI.
  • If the total in Odoo differs from the total on the document, a warning is logged on the bill.
  • Works in Odoo Community: no Enterprise subscription and no Odoo credits needed.

  • The bill stays in draft: always check it before posting.
