How it works

1. Your team logs its hours.

Timesheets on the project, and an hourly cost on each employee.

2. Invoices and bills carry the project.

On each line of a customer invoice or a vendor bill, pick the project in the column Analytic Distribution.

3. Open the project.

The tab Profitability shows hours spent, timesheet cost, revenue, other costs, margin and margin %.

Settings

Where: Project › Configuration › Settings › Analytics › Analytic Accounting

SettingWhat it doesDefault
Analytic AccountingOdoo's own option. It shows the column Analytic Distribution on invoice and bill lines. Switch it on once: without it you cannot link an invoice to a project, and the revenue stays at 0.Off

Odoo also has this box in the Invoicing settings, but shows it there to users with full accounting rights only. The module adds it to the Project settings. The tab Profitability is shown to project managers only. The project list also gets the columns Hours spent and Margin.

Good to know

  • The timesheet cost uses the hourly cost of each employee. Without hourly cost, the hours cost 0.
  • Revenue and other costs come from the analytic account of the project. An invoice or a bill without that analytic distribution is not counted.
  • Only project managers see the figures.

Upgrading from version 17.0.1.0.x

Replace the code, then upgrade on the command line, before anyone signs in, all the modules of this suite that are installed, in one go:

odoo-bin -d <database> -u <module>,<module> --stop-after-init

Three modules of the suite add fields to contacts and companies. Until they are upgraded, Odoo refuses the sign-in.

  • The box Analytic Accounting in the Project settings is new.
  • The figures are now reserved to project managers in every case. Before, they were hidden on the screens, but a project user could still read them through the API.
