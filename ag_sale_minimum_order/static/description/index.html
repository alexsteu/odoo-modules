Free module for Odoo 18 · Sales

Odoo refuses to confirm a sales order below the minimum amount you set, and shows both amounts.

For companies that do not accept small orders.

Tested on Odoo 18 Community · 6 automated tests · English and French · LGPL-3
By Alexandre Gabriel, independent Odoo developer. Need it adapted to your company? See below.

The message when someone confirms an order that is too small: “S00209 cannot be confirmed: its untaxed amount is $ 89.00 and the minimum order amount is $ 500.00.” Demo data.

How it works

1. Set your minimum.

One untaxed amount, in the Sales settings.

2. Someone confirms a small order.

Odoo blocks the confirmation and shows the amount of the order and the minimum.

3. Decide.

Add products to the order, or change the minimum.

Settings

Where: Sales › Configuration › Settings › Quotations & Orders › Minimum order amount

SettingWhat it doesDefault
Minimum order amountUntaxed amount below which an order cannot be confirmed. 0 means no minimum.0

The setting, in Sales. Demo data.

Good to know

  • One minimum for the whole company. Not one per customer.
  • The untaxed amount is compared. For an order in another currency, the minimum is converted into that currency.

Upgrading from version 18.0.1.0.x

Replace the code, then upgrade on the command line, before anyone signs in, all the modules of this suite that are installed, in one go:

odoo-bin -d <database> -u <module>,<module> --stop-after-init

Three modules of the suite add fields to contacts and companies. Until they are upgraded, Odoo refuses the sign-in.
