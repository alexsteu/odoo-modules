Free module for Odoo 17 · Sales

Each line of a quotation or sales order gets a number: on screen, on the PDF and on the online quotation.

For companies whose customers refer to a line by its number, on the phone or in their purchase order.

How it works

1. Install the module.

There is nothing to set up.

2. Open a quotation or a sales order.

The column # numbers the product lines.

3. Print or send it.

The numbers are also on the PDF and on the online quotation.

The column # on the quotation report that Odoo prints, shown here in its HTML preview. This quotation is not the one above. Demo data, shown in the Odoo 18 version.

Settings

Nothing to configure.

Good to know

  • Sections and notes are not numbered.
  • The numbers follow the order of the lines. Move a line and the numbers change.
  • Sales documents only: quotations and sales orders. Not invoices or delivery slips.

Upgrading from version 17.0.1.0.x

Replace the code, then upgrade on the command line, before anyone signs in, all the modules of this suite that are installed, in one go:

odoo-bin -d <database> -u <module>,<module> --stop-after-init

Three modules of the suite add fields to contacts and companies. Until they are upgraded, Odoo refuses the sign-in.
