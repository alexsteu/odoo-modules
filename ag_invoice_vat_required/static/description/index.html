Free module for Odoo 17 · Invoicing

Odoo refuses to post a customer invoice to a company with no Tax ID (VAT number), and says what to do.

For companies that must show the VAT number of their business customers on invoices.

The message when you post an invoice to a company without Tax ID: “Atelier Dubois SA is a company but has no Tax ID / VAT number. Add it on the contact before posting this invoice, or tick "No Tax ID needed" on the contact if this company has none.” Demo data, shown in the Odoo 18 version.

How it works

1. Post a customer invoice.

If the customer is a company without Tax ID, Odoo blocks the posting: from the invoice, and from the list with Actions › Post entries. The same goes for customer credit notes.

2. Fix the contact.

Add the Tax ID on the contact. If this company has none, tick No Tax ID needed.

3. Post again.

The invoice is posted as usual.

On the contact: the box No Tax ID needed, for a company that really has none. Demo data, shown in the Odoo 18 version.

Settings

No general setting. The box No Tax ID needed is on the contact form. It is shown for companies that have no Tax ID.

Good to know

  • Individuals are not affected. For a person attached to a company, the company is checked.
  • The module checks that a Tax ID is filled in. It does not check that the number is valid.
  • Vendor bills are not affected.
  • An invoice that Odoo posts by itself at its date goes through the same check: it stays in draft and Odoo writes the reason on the invoice.

Upgrading from version 17.0.1.0.x

Replace the code, then upgrade on the command line, before anyone signs in, all the modules of this suite that are installed, in one go:

odoo-bin -d <database> -u <module>,<module> --stop-after-init

Three modules of the suite add fields to contacts and companies. Until they are upgraded, Odoo refuses the sign-in.

  • The check now also applies to the list action and to the invoices Odoo posts by itself. Before, only the Confirm button of the invoice was covered.
  • The box No Tax ID needed is new: tick it on the companies that really have no Tax ID.
