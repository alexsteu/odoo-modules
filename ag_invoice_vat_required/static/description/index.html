Odoo refuses to post a customer invoice to a company with no Tax ID (VAT number), and says what to do.

The message when you post an invoice to a company without Tax ID: “Atelier Dubois SA is a company but has no Tax ID / VAT number. Add it on the contact before posting this invoice, or tick "No Tax ID needed" on the contact if this company has none.” Demo data.

How it works

1. Post a customer invoice.

If the customer is a company without Tax ID, Odoo blocks the posting. The same goes for customer credit notes.

2. Fix the contact.

Add the Tax ID on the contact. If this company has none, tick No Tax ID needed.

3. Post again.

The invoice is posted as usual.

On the contact: the box No Tax ID needed, for a company that really has none. Demo data.

Settings

No general setting. The box No Tax ID needed is on the contact form. It is shown for companies that have no Tax ID.

Good to know

  • Individuals are not affected. For a person attached to a company, the company is checked.
  • The module checks that a Tax ID is filled in. It does not check that the number is valid.
  • Vendor bills are not affected.
