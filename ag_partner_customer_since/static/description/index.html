How it works

1. Install the module.

There is nothing to set up.

2. Open a contact.

In the tab Sales & Purchase, the line Customer since shows the date of the first confirmed sales order.

3. Companies and their contacts.

For a company, the orders of all its contacts count too.

Settings

Nothing to configure.

Good to know

  • The line is hidden as long as the contact has no confirmed sales order.
  • The date comes from the sales orders in Odoo. A customer you only invoiced, without a sales order, has no date.
  • Users without access to Sales can read the date too.
  • With several companies in one Odoo, only the orders of the companies you have selected count.

Upgrading from version 17.0.1.0.x

Replace the code, then upgrade on the command line, before anyone signs in, all the modules of this suite that are installed, in one go:

odoo-bin -d <database> -u <module>,<module> --stop-after-init

Three modules of the suite add fields to contacts and companies. Until they are upgraded, Odoo refuses the sign-in.
