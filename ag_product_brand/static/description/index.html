How it works

1. Create your brands.

In Sales › Configuration › Brands: a name, and if you wish a logo and a description.

2. Choose the brand on each product.

The field Brand is on the product form.

3. Search and group by brand.

In the product list, and in the sales analysis with Group By › Brand.

The Brand field on the product form. Demo data, shown in the Odoo 18 version.

Settings

Where: Sales › Configuration › Brands

Only sales managers can create or change brands.

Good to know

  • One brand per product.
  • Two brands cannot have the same name.
  • The brand is not printed on quotations or invoices.
