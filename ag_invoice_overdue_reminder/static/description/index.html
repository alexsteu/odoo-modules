Overdue Invoice Reminder

Get paid faster, without writing a single reminder by hand

Free module by an independent Odoo developer. Need it adapted to your company? See below.

Odoo Community has no automatic payment follow-up. This module adds a simple one: every day it e-mails customers whose invoice is past its due date.

  • Only posted customer invoices that are not paid or partially paid and past their due date.
  • A reminder every X days (7 by default), at most N times (3 by default).
  • The e-mail shows the open amount and a button to view and pay the invoice online. It is logged in the chatter.
  • Turn it off for one invoice with No automatic reminder (Other Info tab).
  • Editable e-mail template: Invoicing: overdue invoice reminder.

Setup

Invoicing > Configuration > Settings > Customer Payments > Overdue invoice reminders.
