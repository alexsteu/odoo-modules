Free module for Odoo 18 · Sales

For customers who require a purchase order number, Odoo refuses to confirm the order until the Customer Reference is filled in.

For suppliers whose customers reject an invoice that does not show their PO number.

Tested on Odoo 18 Community · 6 automated tests · English and French · LGPL-3
By Alexandre Gabriel, independent Odoo developer. Need it adapted to your company? See below.

The message when the Customer Reference is missing: “Menuiserie Rochat Sàrl requires a PO number. Please fill in the Customer Reference of S00210 before confirming.” Demo data.

How it works

1. Tick the box on the customer.

Requires a PO number, in the tab Sales & Purchase.

2. Someone confirms an order without reference.

Odoo blocks the confirmation and names the customer and the order.

3. Fill in the Customer Reference.

Then confirm again.

Settings

No general setting. The box Requires a PO number is on each customer, in the tab Sales & Purchase.

On the customer, Sales & Purchase tab: the box Requires a PO number. The line Customer since comes from another module of this suite. Demo data.

Good to know

  • The box on a company also applies to the orders of its contacts.
  • A reference made of spaces only counts as empty.
  • The module checks that a reference is filled in. It cannot check that it is the right one.

Upgrading from version 18.0.1.0.x

Replace the code, then upgrade on the command line, before anyone signs in, all the modules of this suite that are installed, in one go:

odoo-bin -d <database> -u <module>,<module> --stop-after-init

Three modules of the suite add fields to contacts and companies. Until they are upgraded, Odoo refuses the sign-in.
