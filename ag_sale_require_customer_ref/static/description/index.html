Require Customer PO Number

Never send an invoice without the customer's PO number again

  • Tick Requires a PO number on a customer (Sales & Purchase tab).
  • Its sales orders can no longer be confirmed while the Customer Reference is empty.
  • Works for the company and all its contacts.
  • Avoids rejected invoices at large customers and public bodies.
